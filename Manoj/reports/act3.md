# ACT 3: Is it People or Machines?

## What I did
- Made a guess about how people behave in the system.
- Checked if passenger counts are higher in evening peak hours than late-night hours.
- Also checked if the same pattern could just be bad data or logging noise.

## My guess
- Main idea: passengers behave differently at peak times, so the system is driven by real demand.
- Other idea: it might just be a data problem, not real behavior.

## What the code looked at
- Average passenger count from 5pm to 8pm.
- Average passenger count from midnight to 5am.
- A simple stats test to compare them.
- A check to see whether weird data points happen at certain hours.

## Notes
This act is about not jumping to one answer. It checks one story and then asks: could it just be data noise?
