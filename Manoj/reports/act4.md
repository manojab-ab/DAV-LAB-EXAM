# ACT 4: City Rhythm

## What I did
- Grouped clean trips by day and by hour.
- Looked at how many trips happen, average fare, and average distance.
- Shuffled the data to see if the patterns stay real or just happen by chance.

## How I grouped things
- Daily summary: count of trips, average fare, average distance.
- Hourly summary: count of trips, average fare.
- Random baseline: mix up the data to check if patterns disappear.

## Notes
This act is about whether the city has a real rhythm or if the numbers are just noise. It checks which patterns survive a simple randomness test.
