# ACT 5: What If the System Changes?

## What I did
- Used our seed number `R = 7` to make a simple change.
- Simulated two cases:
  1. Fares go up by 7%.
  2. Trip volume drops by 7%.
- Checked how revenue changes.

## How the simulation works
- For fares: increase prices by 7% and recalc money.
- For volume: drop the number of trips by 7% and see what happens.

## Notes
This part is not about a perfect forecast. It is just to see whether the system can handle a small shock or if it changes the results more than expected.
